The editor has six **docks**, as in IntelliJ: left top, left bottom, right top, right bottom, bottom left and bottom right. Their panels are icons in the **stripes** on the edges of the window (hover one for its name):

- the left stripe holds the left top dock, then, under a line, the left bottom dock, and at its foot the bottom left dock;
- the right stripe holds the right docks the same way, and the bottom right dock at its foot.

The layout is saved with the project and restored when you come back.

## Show and hide panels

- **Click an icon** to show its panel in its dock. Click it again to hide the dock.
- The panel's header has its title, a **⋮** menu and **Hide** (**−**).
- _View › Docks_ shows or hides a whole dock, and _View › Panels_ shows a panel that was closed.

## Move a panel

- **Drag its icon** to another group of a stripe, or between the icons of a group to reorder them. You can also drag a panel by its header, onto a stripe or a dock. Zones light up to show where it will land.
- Drag it **out of the docks** to make a **floating** window. Drag it by its title, resize it by its edges, and drag its tab back to a dock to dock it again.
- **Right-click an icon** (or the header) for _Hide_, _Close_, _Close others_, _Float_ and _Move to_ (a dock).

## Resize

- Drag the line between two docks, or between a dock and the screen.

## Focus with the keyboard

`F6` moves the focus to the next part of the editor (a dock, the screen), and `Shift+F6` to the previous one.

## Layouts

_View › Layouts_:

- **Save layout** keeps the current arrangement under a name;
- the saved layouts are listed there: choose one to switch to it;
- **Reset layout** goes back to the default.

Moving, closing, floating and resizing a panel are steps you can undo: `Ctrl+Z` with the focus on the layout, or in the **History** panel under _Layout_.

## While playing

With _Settings › Editor › Game view › Maximize on play_, the docks and stripes hide while the game plays and come back when it stops.

## Theme

_View › Toggle light/dark theme_, or _Settings › Appearance_ to choose a theme. Plugins can add themes.
